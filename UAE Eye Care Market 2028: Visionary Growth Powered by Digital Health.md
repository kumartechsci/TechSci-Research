<p>Additionally, online retail channels are growing rapidly and offer customized solutions via virtual trial-ons as well as AI-powered prescriptions.&nbsp;E-commerce platforms such as Amazon UAE and Noon.com have made it easier to access prescription products for eyewear and lenses particularly in smaller cities like the emirates.&nbsp;With a tech-savvy, young population that is the UAE Eye Care Market is well-positioned to benefit from digital change.</p>
