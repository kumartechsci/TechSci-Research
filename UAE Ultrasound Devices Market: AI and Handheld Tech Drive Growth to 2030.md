<p>Another trend that is gaining momentum is the blending of 3D and 4D imaging, especially in the field of prenatal diagnostics and cardiology.&nbsp;The latest imaging technologies help clinicians gain greater insight into anatomy and enhancing the engagement of patients.&nbsp;Tele-ultrasound technology is becoming more popular, particularly in remote locations and at-home healthcare settings which allow for instant consultations and diagnosis.</p>
