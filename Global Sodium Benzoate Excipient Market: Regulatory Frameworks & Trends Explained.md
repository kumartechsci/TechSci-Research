<p><strong>Understanding Sodium Benzoate as an Excipient</strong></p>
<p>Sodium benzoate is a widely used preservative excipient in the pharmaceutical and food industries. It prevents the growth of bacteria, yeast, and fungi, making it critical for extending the shelf life of products. As an excipient in pharmaceuticals, it is especially important in liquid dosage forms, such as syrups, oral suspensions, and pediatric formulations.</p>
<p>In the pharmaceutical space, sodium benzoate's role goes beyond preservation. It influences drug stability, bioavailability, and compatibility with other ingredients. The rising demand for safe, effective, and long-lasting medications has led to increased investments in the R&amp;D of sodium benzoate formulations.</p>
