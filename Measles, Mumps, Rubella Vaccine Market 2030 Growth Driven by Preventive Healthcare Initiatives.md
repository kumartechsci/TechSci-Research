<ul>
<li>Educating parents on vaccination schedules.</li>
<li>Providing reminders for follow-up doses.</li>
<li>Addressing vaccine hesitancy through credible information.</li>
</ul>
<p>These tech-enabled interventions are proving effective in increasing vaccination rates and generating long-term demand for MMR vaccines.</p>
<p><strong>Expanding Adult Immunization and Booster Dose Programs</strong></p>
<p>While the MMR vaccine is primarily associated with childhood immunization, adult vaccination programs are emerging as a significant growth opportunity. Research indicates that immunity against measles, mumps, and rubella may wane in adulthood, especially for those vaccinated decades ago.</p>
<p>Public health authorities are encouraging booster shots for:</p>
<ul>
<li>Healthcare workers exposed to infectious diseases.</li>
<li>Teachers and education professionals in constant contact with children.</li>
<li>International travelers visiting outbreak-prone regions.</li>
</ul>
<p>Private clinics, corporate health programs, and occupational health providers are integrating MMR vaccines into adult preventive care packages, expanding the vaccine&rsquo;s target audience.</p>
