<p><strong>Government Initiatives Boosting Market Growth</strong></p>
<p>The Indian government has launched several initiatives to strengthen cancer care and drive the radiotherapy devices market. Programs like the National Cancer Control Programme (NCCP) focus on prevention, early detection, and treatment infrastructure. The establishment of specialized cancer care centers across rural and underserved regions has improved access to advanced treatments.</p>
<p>The Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PMJAY), under Ayushman Bharat, provides financial support for cancer treatment, including radiotherapy. This has made treatment more affordable for low-income families. Government funding for healthcare infrastructure has also encouraged private sector participation, creating a favorable environment for advanced device adoption.</p>

<p><strong>Role of Private Healthcare Sector</strong></p>
<p>India&rsquo;s private healthcare sector is a major contributor to the growth of radiotherapy devices. Rising disposable incomes and demand for better healthcare services have pushed private hospitals and cancer centers to invest in advanced radiotherapy systems. Modern techniques such as image-guided radiotherapy (IGRT), intensity-modulated radiotherapy (IMRT), and stereotactic body radiotherapy (SBRT) are increasingly available in urban and semi-urban hospitals.</p>
<p>Private players, especially in metropolitan cities, are actively upgrading infrastructure to meet rising cancer care needs. Their investment in technology and facilities complements government programs, further driving market expansion.</p>

<p><strong>Application Segment</strong></p>
<p>Breast Cancer emerged as the dominant application segment in 2024. Breast cancer remains one of the most common cancers among women in India, which has significantly driven demand for radiotherapy. Advances in screening, awareness campaigns, and early detection have led to more patients seeking treatment.</p>
<p>Modern radiotherapy methods like IMRT and IGRT have improved patient survival rates and reduced side effects. Their effectiveness in both post-surgery and advanced-stage cases has strengthened the role of radiotherapy as a key treatment option for breast cancer.</p>
<p><strong>Regional Segment</strong></p>
<p>West India has emerged as the fastest-growing region in 2024. Cities such as Mumbai, Pune, Ahmedabad, and Jaipur are investing heavily in healthcare infrastructure. The presence of leading private healthcare providers and specialized cancer centers has further fueled demand for radiotherapy devices. Awareness about cancer treatment and prevention has also increased adoption of radiotherapy in this region.</p>
